# Changelog

What changed in each release of Grimoire. Downloads are on the
[Releases](https://github.com/SecreSwalowtail/Grimoire/releases) page.

## 1.0.0

The first release.

- Scan cards with the camera, one at a time or a whole stack in Bulk mode.
- Binders with their value and its change over a week, a month or a year.
- Market: search every Magic card, with filters for format, colors, mana cost, set, rarity and
  language.
- Card detail with Cardmarket and TCGplayer prices, price history, printings, rules and legality.
- Decks and folders with stats, a legality check per format, and drag and drop.
- Prices in your currency.
- Import from Manabox, export your collection as CSV.
